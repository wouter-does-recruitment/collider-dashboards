Working hypothesis

Where this CTO search should probably go next.

1. Stents / cathetersStart with people around vascular devices, percutaneous systems, urinary devices, implantables and catheter-based products.
2. MDR / ISO 13485BraidStent needs someone who understands regulatory file reality, risk management, PMS/PMCF/CER, V&V and clinical validation.
3. Product to manufacturingPrioritise profiles who have moved devices from design into manufacturing, validation, suppliers and serial production.
4. BiomaterialsUse microfluidics / biomaterials / biodegradable polymer people as adjacency, especially if the degradation / material pathway becomes critical.
