Market-map logic

The pool is intentionally broader than direct candidates only. The exact search niche is small, so the best route is to combine candidate leads with people who can unlock the local Valencia and Spanish female-health ecosystem.

Direct candidate potential

Could possibly take a CEO / Co-Founder, operator, or venture-building role. Needs validation on founder energy, fundraising appetite, and availability.

Referral / ecosystem nodes

May not be candidates, but can point to hidden founders, clinicians, operators, investors, university spin-offs and female-health communities.

Benchmark profiles

Strong examples of what “good” looks like in women’s health, diagnostics or MedTech. Useful for calibration and warm-intro routes.
