Why the status model changes

A flat status like "replied" is not enough. Replies now mean different things: interested, open to call, advisor only, referral, not now or needs qualification.

  • Main status: outreach sent, replied, call link sent, call scheduled, staff interview, matchmaking, parked.
  • Reply type: interested, asked for details, advisor only, needs qualification, referral angle, declined.
  • Next action: send JD, send Calendly, wait for booking, ask CV, share with Consuelo, park or drop.
